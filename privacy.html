Privacy Policy

My Expense Ledger — Built by John-de-Scholar  ·  Last updated: July 2026

1. What We Collect

We collect the information you provide during registration: your username, full name, date of birth, phone number (hashed), and email address. We also store your financial entries (expenses and income) that you input into the app.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide the expense tracking service. We do not sell, share, or transfer your personal data to third parties. Your financial data is stored securely in Google Firebase Firestore under your unique user ID.

3. Data Storage & Security

All data is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore). Your PIN is hashed and never stored in plain text. Biometric credentials are stored locally on your device only and never transmitted to our servers.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

— Google Firebase (data storage and authentication)
— EmailJS (support ticket delivery)
— Google Analytics (anonymous usage statistics)

5. Your Rights

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from the Manage tab → Data & Account → Delete My Account. Upon deletion, all your data is permanently removed from our servers within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

This app is intended for users aged 16 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.

8. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact us at: myfinanceledgerapp@gmail.com
