Privacy Policy

My Expense Ledger — Built by John-de-Scholar  ·  Last updated: July 2026 (v2.5.0)

1. What We Collect

We collect the information you provide during registration: your full name, username, date of birth, country, and email address. We also store your financial entries (expenses and income) that you input into the app. No phone number is collected or stored.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide the expense tracking service. We do not sell, share, or transfer your personal data to third parties. Your financial data is stored securely in Google Firebase Firestore under your unique user ID. Your email address is used for account creation, PIN recovery, and duplicate account detection only.

3. Data Storage & Security

All data is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore). Your PIN must be exactly 6 digits and is hashed using a one-way algorithm before storage — it is never stored in plain text. You may sign up and sign in using Google Sign-In for a faster, passwordless experience. PIN reset is handled via a 6-digit code sent to your registered email address, valid for 5 minutes.

4. Authentication & Account Recovery

If you forget your PIN, tap "Forgot your PIN?" on the login screen and enter your registered email address. A 6-digit reset code will be sent to your inbox — it expires in 5 minutes. Enter the code in the app, then set a new 6-digit PIN. Your email is the only recovery method — keep it accurate in your profile.

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

— Google Firebase (data storage and authentication)
— Google Sign-In (optional sign-in method)
— EmailJS (PIN reset codes and support ticket delivery)
— Google Analytics (anonymous usage statistics)

6. Your Rights

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from the Manage tab → Delete Account. Upon deletion, all your personal data — expenses, income, settings, registry entry, and credentials — is permanently and irreversibly removed from our servers immediately. This data cannot be recovered.

7. Children's Privacy

This app is intended for users aged 16 and above, in line with GDPR recommendations. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16. Users under 16 may use Guest Mode where no personal data is sent to our servers.

8. Session Security & Auto-Lock

The app automatically locks after 10 minutes of inactivity. Local storage is cleared when you log out or delete your account. The app uses your device's local storage to remember your session, active tab, and preferences — no tracking cookies are used.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. All users will be required to re-read and re-accept updated terms upon next login after a policy change.

10. Governing Law

These Terms and Privacy Policy are governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria (Nigeria Data Protection Regulation — NDPR) and comply with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) of the European Union where applicable.

11. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact us at: myfinanceledgerapp@gmail.com. We aim to respond within 48 hours.
